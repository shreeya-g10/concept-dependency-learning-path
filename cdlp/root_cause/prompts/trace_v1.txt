A wrong answer can come from a gap in the question's own concept, or from a gap further back in
one of its prerequisites. Decide which concept most likely caused THIS wrong answer.

Use two kinds of evidence:
1. The wrong answer itself: what misconception would lead a student to choose exactly this option?
   Which concept in the chain does that misconception belong to?
2. The student's mastery of each concept (0 = does not know it, 1 = knows it well, "unknown" = no
   information). A prerequisite the student has clearly mastered is an unlikely cause. A
   prerequisite with low mastery is a likely cause only if the wrong answer fits a gap in it.
Low mastery alone is not enough: if the wrong answer shows a mistake that belongs to the question's
own concept, blame that concept even if a prerequisite has low mastery.

You may ONLY rank concepts from the prerequisite chain below, using their exact ids.
