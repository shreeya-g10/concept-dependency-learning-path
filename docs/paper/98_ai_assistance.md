# Disclosure of AI Assistance

Parts of the software for this project were written with the help of an AI coding assistant (Anthropic's Claude, used through Claude Code). The assistant drafted and revised code, tests, configuration and documentation under the authors' direction. The authors reviewed, ran and tested every change before merging it. The assistant also helped draft parts of this manuscript's text, and the authors checked that text against the code and data. All research design decisions, gold annotations, the choice of evaluation protocols, and the interpretation of results are the authors' own. No AI system was used to produce or alter gold labels, and every citation was checked by the authors against the original source. The LLMs used *as components of the method* (open-weight models run locally through Ollama) are described in Sections 3–5 and are separate from this assistance.
